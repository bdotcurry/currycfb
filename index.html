UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

What the numbers mean

01 / RATING

Predictive Rating

Composite power rating expressed on a point-spread scale. Higher indicates a stronger team. A 5-point difference suggests a roughly five-point neutral-site edge, based on the model.

02 / TCS

Title Contender Score

Composite measure of how closely a team matches the statistical profile of successful College Football Playoff-era teams. No team with a score <96.0 has won the National Title.

03 / SOR

Strength of Record

Schedule-strength measure covering the opponents a team has played to date.

04 / SOS

Strength of Schedule

Schedule-strength measure covering the full regular-season schedule, including remaining opponents.

Game performance metrics are sourced from GameOnPaper.
