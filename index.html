UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

What the numbers mean

01 / RATING

Predictive Rating

Your composite power rating expressed on a point-spread scale. Higher indicates a stronger team. A 5-point difference suggests a roughly five-point neutral-site edge, based on the model.

02 / TCS

Title Contender Score

Your composite measure of how closely a team matches the statistical profile of successful College Football Playoff-era teams.

03 / SOR

Strength of Record

Your schedule-adjusted measure of what a team has accomplished in games already played.

04 / SOS

Strength of Schedule

Your schedule-strength measure covering the full regular-season schedule, including remaining opponents.

Game performance metrics are sourced from GameOnPaper. Custom scores and ratings come from your Excel model. “O” denotes offense, “D” denotes defense.
